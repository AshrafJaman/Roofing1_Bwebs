Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide free written quotations?
Yes, we provide free, no-obligation quotes for all residential and commercial roofing projects.
Which areas of London do you cover?
We are based in South West London (Putney) and cover all surrounding areas including Surrey and Central London.
Is your work guaranteed and insured?
Absolutely. We are fully insured and all our new installations come with a comprehensive guarantee for your peace of mind.
Do you handle emergency roof repairs?
Yes, we offer emergency call-out services for urgent leaks and storm damage. Call us immediately on 0208 485 9967.
Do I need planning permission for a new roof?
Usually no, unless it's a listed building or conservation area. We can advise you on this during our consultation.
